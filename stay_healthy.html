☀️ Sunlight & Mumbai Context

  • Morning (7AM): Critical for circadian rhythm. 10-20 min direct sky exposure.
  • Midday (Break): 15 min arms/legs exposure for Vitamin D (UV strong in Mumbai).
  • Evening: Avoid bright light after 8 PM to protect melatonin.
  • Hydration: Mumbai humidity requires extra water + electrolytes (pinch of salt).

🍽️ OMAD Meal Rules (8 PM)

  • Protein First: Eat meat/eggs/dairy first (Target 100g+).
  • Carbs: Sweet potato, quinoa, rice (Fuel recovery).
  • Veggies: Large volume (Fiber for gut health).
  • Fats: Olive oil, avocado, nuts (Hormone support).
  • Supplements: Vit D3+K2 (if winter/deficient), Magnesium (before bed).

⚠️ Critical Success Factors

  • Sleep Quality: 6 hours is minimum. Protect it fiercely (Blackout curtains).
  • Progressive Overload: Track reps/tempo weekly. Increase difficulty slowly.
  • Kegels: 30/day total (Morning + Breaks). Include reverse kegels.
  • Consistency: 80% effort sustained > 100% effort burned out.

Sunday is fully OFF. Rest & Reset.
